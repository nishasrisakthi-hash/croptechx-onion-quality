WHY THIS MATTERS

The Problem with Conventional Onion Grading

OFFICIAL SIH PROBLEM STATEMENT

“Quality assessment and grading of onions are often subjective and vary across procurement centers, resulting in disputes and inconsistencies.”

⚖️

Subjective Assessment

Different procurement centres may arrive at different grading decisions, creating inconsistency in quality assessment.

🤝

Disputes & Trust Gap

Inconsistent grading can create disagreements between farmers, procurement centres and buyers.

📦

Information Gap

Packaged produce may not expose useful information about quality, origin, handling and expected shelf life.

🔗

Limited Traceability

Farmer, sowing, harvest and market-arrival information can be difficult for consumers to access directly.
